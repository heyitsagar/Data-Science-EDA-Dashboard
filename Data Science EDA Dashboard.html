Welcome to the Interactive Data Pipeline

This dashboard simulates a complete end-to-end Python Data Science workflow directly in your browser. We start by generating a simulated, messy "Retail Customer" dataset. Through the navigation steps, you will see how data is cleaned, how missing values and outliers are statistically treated, visualized, and finally prepared for Machine Learning with engineered features.

1. Messy Data Injection

Synthetic nulls, negative ages, and duplicate records are dynamically generated.

2. Statistical Imputation

Median and standard IQR calculations are applied to clean the dataset.

3. Insights & Engineering

New predictive columns are derived and visually validated.
